# Click matrix

Baseline: 2026-10-04. A starting inventory of primary user interactions, including text entry, selection, toggles, and keyboard activation. Cross-reference the [feature list](FEATURE_LIST.md), [customization list](CUSTOMIZATION_LIST.md), and [feature matrix](FEATURE_MATRIX.md). IDs remain stable as coverage grows.

Rows under Current interactions describe inspected code. **B1** and **B2** identify assertions in [workspace.spec.ts](../../tests/e2e/workspace.spec.ts): B1 is starter/source/read/save/customize/profile; B2 is posts/conversations/filtering/mobile. **B3** and **B4** identify [pages.spec.ts](../../tests/e2e/pages.spec.ts): B3 is isolated layout previews/search/history/edits/mobile; B4 is local directory/customization/profile/settings routes and reload. **Gap** means a dedicated interaction assertion is missing or incomplete. This inventory is a source review, not a new browser run; historical results are in [IMPLEMENTATION.md](IMPLEMENTATION.md).

## Routes and persistence

The workspace uses `/feeds`, `/feeds/:id`, `/feeds/:id/saved`, `/feeds/:id/customize`, `/connections?tab=:connectionId` (legacy background URLs open customization), `/profile`, and `/settings?tab=ai` (legacy `?tab=appearance` renders AI configuration). Route generation is in [navigation.ts](../../src/navigation.ts). Reader query, saved/unread filters, open conversations, expanded text, and warning reveal are component state; they are not saved feed preferences. Saves below use the local API. Labeled `/preview/...` pages use temporary sample state and cannot validate a real source or paid AI call.
